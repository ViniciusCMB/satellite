7th Latin American Space Challenge

The largest rocket and satellite engineering competition in the world

The 2026 LASC takes place September 2–5, 2026 at the Sugarcane Launch Range (SLR) in Iacanga, São Paulo, Brazil. The event brings together university teams from across Latin America and beyond for three competitions: the Rocket Challenge (apogee categories from 500 m to 3,000 m plus hybrid/liquid), the Satellite Challenge (PocketQube, CanSat and CubeSat form factors), and the new Lander Challenge.

The Helike #213 competes in the Satellite Challenge (PocketQube) aboard the Dédalo #11 in the Rocket Challenge — a full mission entry, from liftoff to SRAB recovery. This is Serra Rocketry's return to the launch range after LASC 2025, with a payload built to answer one question: can recovery be engineered to never fail?
